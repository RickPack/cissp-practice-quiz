        Commute mode

        Study mode with answers, not a practice exam. You hear whether each answer is right, the correct answer, and the explanation. Nothing here is scored or saved to your practice session or report. Your answers only update your review deck.

        • Each question and its options are read aloud. Then say A, B, C, or D. Say repeat, skip (or next), or stop at any time it is listening.
        • If you say nothing for about 8 seconds, the question is skipped and the answer is read.
        • Keep the screen on and this tab in front: it pauses when the screen locks or you switch apps, and starts the question again when you come back.

        Voice answers are not available in this browser. Commute mode still reads each question, pauses about 8 seconds, then reads the answer. Tap an answer or press Skip to go faster.
